🎓 Promotion / new session
Once a year, in a holiday. Part 1 – Start new session (once): backup, archive copy, unpaid fees carried forward, the new session starts. Part 2 – Promote classes (in batches): last year's results saved, students moved, roll numbers, the year's attendance / marks of those classes emptied. Classes still in exams can be ticked "still finishing" and promoted later. Everything is shown first as a preview; nothing changes until you type the session name and tap Start. Practise on a COPY of the sheet first.
